Local Guest Guide

Clearwater Beach & Indian Rocks Beach

America's best beaches, world-famous Gulf sunsets, fresh seafood, and the ultimate Florida beach lifestyle

Where to Eat Best Beaches Activities Nightlife Getting Around Family Tips Rainy Day Local Tips FAQ

Welcome to one of Florida's most beloved stretches of Gulf Coast — Clearwater Beach and Indian Rocks Beach in Pinellas County. Whether you're chasing powder-white sand and warm turquoise water, looking for the freshest stone crab claws on the coast, or just want to watch the sun melt into the Gulf every evening, this barrier island corridor delivers all of it and more. We're local hosts who live and breathe this area, and this guide covers everything you need to make the most of your stay.

From the lively beachfront of Clearwater Beach with its iconic Pier 60 to the quieter, walkable charm of Indian Rocks Beach and Indian Shores, every day here feels like a vacation postcard. Nearby Dunedin and Tarpon Springs add day-trip flavor — and Tampa and St. Pete are both under an hour away when you want big-city energy.

Where to Eat

Best Restaurants Near Clearwater Beach & Indian Rocks Beach

Iconic Beach Bar
Frenchy's Rockaway Grill
Clearwater Beach institution since 1981. Fresh grouper sandwiches, cold beer, and feet-in-the-sand dining that defines the Gulf Coast experience.
📍 7 Rockaway St, Clearwater Beach
Waterfront Seafood
The Salty Pelican
Relaxed waterfront bar with excellent fresh seafood and gorgeous sunset views over the Intracoastal Waterway — a true Indian Rocks Beach gem.
📍 Georgia Ave, Indian Rocks Beach
Gulf Coast Seafood Landmark
Crabby Bill's
A Gulf Coast tradition — fresh local seafood, stone crab claws in season, and ice-cold beer right on the water. One of the best seafood spots on the beach.
📍 401 Gulf Blvd, Indian Rocks Beach
Historic Beachfront Dining
Palm Pavilion
Clearwater Beach's oldest beach bar (open since 1926) with a full menu, frozen drinks, and you truly cannot get any closer to the sand.
📍 10 Bay Esplanade, Clearwater Beach
Italian on Clearwater Beach
Sea-Guini
Upscale Italian right on Clearwater Beach — housemade pasta, fresh Gulf seafood, and a romantic atmosphere perfect for a special dinner.
📍 483 Mandalay Ave, Clearwater Beach
Beloved Local Café
Clear Sky Cafe
A beloved local spot with a great all-day breakfast, fresh seafood plates, and a wraparound porch that's perfect for people-watching on Mandalay.
📍 490 Mandalay Ave, Clearwater Beach

🦞 Local Food Tip

Stone crab season runs October through May — it's the Gulf Coast's finest delicacy and you shouldn't leave without trying a plate. Crabby Bill's and The Salty Pelican serve them fresh with mustard sauce. For the quintessential Clearwater Beach meal, grab a grouper sandwich from Frenchy's and eat it with your feet in the sand. It never gets old.

🌅 Sunset Tip

Pier 60 at Clearwater Beach hosts a nightly Sunset Celebration with street performers, artisans, and live musicians — it starts two hours before sunset and is completely free. Indian Rocks Beach sunsets are equally stunning and far less crowded. The Intracoastal side of the islands faces east and is beautiful at sunrise too — kayak it early for dolphin sightings.

Home of Winter the Dolphin. An inspiring marine rescue facility with up-close animal encounters, touch tanks, and an unforgettable story of recovery and hope.
📍 249 Windward Passage, Clearwater

Gulf Adventures

Florida's fascinating Greek heritage village — authentic Greek restaurants, sponge boats, waterfront shops, and a unique cultural experience unlike anywhere else in Florida.

Water Sports on the Intracoastal
Paddleboard & Kayak Rentals
Rent a paddleboard or kayak and explore the calm Intracoastal waterway behind Indian Rocks Beach. An incredible full-body workout with mangroves, birds, and dolphins.

Nature & Wildlife — Free
Moccasin Lake Nature Park
A beautiful nature center with rescued wildlife, boardwalk nature trails through wetlands, and educational programs. A quiet, underrated local gem in Clearwater.
📍 2750 Park Trail Ln, Clearwater

🐬 Activity Tip

Dolphins are abundant in the Gulf and Intracoastal — you will almost certainly spot them from the beach or on any boat tour. For the best chance, take a morning kayak on the Intracoastal behind Indian Rocks Beach around sunrise. They are incredibly active before 9am and often come within a few feet of paddleboards. Several local outfitters near IRB offer hourly rentals for $15–25/hr.

Getting Around & Parking

Getting Around Clearwater Beach: Transportation & Parking Tips

Best Way to Get Around
The Jolley Trolley
The Jolley Trolley runs the entire Clearwater Beach strip and connects south to Sand Key and Belleair Beach. Just $2.25 per ride or grab a day pass — saves parking headaches entirely.
📍 Multiple stops along Mandalay Ave & Gulf Blvd
Beach Parking
Pier 60 Parking Garage
The main paid parking option on Clearwater Beach, charging around $3/hour with a daily cap. Fills up by 10am in summer — arrive early or use the trolley from off-beach parking lots.
📍 1 Causeway Blvd, Clearwater Beach
Free Beach Parking
Sand Key County Park
One of the only genuinely free parking options near the beach. Sand Key Park has a spacious free lot with a beautiful beach — arrive before 9am on weekends in summer.
📍 1060 Gulf Blvd, Sand Key
Easy Parking at Indian Rocks Beach
IRB Street & Lot Parking
Indian Rocks Beach has far more street parking than Clearwater Beach — many free spots on side streets and a few paid lots near Gulf Blvd. Still, arrive before 10am on peak days.
📍 Side streets off Gulf Blvd, Indian Rocks Beach
Ride-Share
Uber & Lyft
Uber and Lyft are widely available and affordable along the beach corridor. Highly recommended for any nightlife outing. From Indian Rocks Beach to Clearwater Beach typically runs $8–14.
📱 Download before you arrive
Cycling — 75 Miles of Trail
Pinellas Trail
The 75-mile paved Pinellas Trail runs the length of the county. Multiple access points near Indian Rocks Beach and Clearwater Beach make it ideal for car-free exploration of the area.
📍 Multiple access points along Gulf Blvd corridor

🚗 Parking Tip

The number-one mistake visitors make is arriving at Clearwater Beach after 10am on a summer weekend without a parking plan. Beat the rush by arriving before 9am, parking at Sand Key (free!), or simply riding the Jolley Trolley from a free lot on the mainland. Indian Rocks Beach — where most of our rentals are — has dramatically easier parking and a much more relaxed vibe.

Family Tips

Family-Friendly Clearwater Beach & Indian Rocks Beach: Tips for Families

Best Family Beach
Sand Key Park for Young Kids
Sand Key's calm, shallow Gulf entry and less crowded beach make it ideal for young swimmers. Free parking, clean facilities, and a lifeguard on duty in peak season.
📍 1060 Gulf Blvd, Sand Key
Kids' Top Activity
Clearwater Marine Aquarium
Kids absolutely love Winter the dolphin's story. Plan 2–3 hours and consider combo tickets that include a boat tour of Clearwater Harbor. Under 2 is free.
📍 249 Windward Passage, Clearwater
Family Evening Activity
Pier 60 Sunset Celebration
Free, family-safe, and something genuinely exciting every night. Kids love the street performers, sand art competitions, and the energy of the beach at golden hour.
📍 Pier 60, Clearwater Beach — 2 hrs before sunset
Water Adventure for All Ages
Dolphin Boat Tours
A 90-minute dolphin tour from Clearwater Beach Marina is a top memory-maker for families. Kids are almost always guaranteed to see dolphins up close in the wild.
📍 Clearwater Beach Marina — multiple operators
Nature for Curious Kids
Moccasin Lake Nature Park
Rescued owls, raptors, reptiles, and a woodland boardwalk trail make this Clearwater gem a perfect half-morning outing with young children. Very affordable entry.
📍 2750 Park Trail Ln, Clearwater
Day Trip for Families
Caladesi Island Ferry
The ferry to Caladesi Island turns a beach day into a genuine adventure. Older kids and teens love the unspoiled wilderness feel — bring snorkel gear for the crystal-clear Gulf side.
📍 Ferry from Honeymoon Island State Park, Dunedin

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Planning Tip

The Gulf's calm, wave-free water makes it uniquely safe for young kids compared to Atlantic beaches. Water temperature is ideal from May through October. Beat the midday heat by hitting the beach before 11am, retreating for lunch and a rest, then returning around 4pm for the best light and cooler temperatures — plus the best chance of spotting dolphins near the shore.

⛈️ Florida Weather Tip

Summer afternoon storms in Florida are brief and pass quickly — typically 30–60 minutes. Don't cancel a beach day over a morning forecast. The pattern is: beautiful morning, storm rolls through around 3–4pm, clear by 5–6pm for the most spectacular sunset skies of the week. Keep an eye on the Clearwater Beach weather app and stay flexible.

Quick Local Tips

Local Secrets from Your Clearwater Beach & Indian Rocks Beach Hosts

Beat the Beach Parking Rush Arrive at Clearwater Beach by 9am in summer to get a spot. After 10am on weekends, you'll be circling for an hour. Indian Rocks Beach always has easier parking.
Stone Crab Season: October–May Don't visit without trying fresh stone crab claws. Crabby Bill's and The Salty Pelican serve them right — chilled with mustard sauce. It's a Gulf Coast rite of passage.
The Jolley Trolley Saves the Day $2.25 per ride along the whole beach strip. Saves parking, saves money, saves stress. Get a day pass if you're bouncing between Clearwater Beach and Sand Key.
Dolphin Spotting Secret Dolphins are most active on the Intracoastal between 6–9am. Rent a kayak from an IRB outfitter at sunrise and you'll almost certainly share the water with them.
Pier 60 Sunset is Free & Unmissable Every single evening, two hours before sunset. Street performers, music, artists. It's completely free, it's genuinely wonderful, and it never gets old no matter how many times you go.
Caladesi Goes Fast on Weekends The ferry to Caladesi Island has limited daily capacity. On summer weekends it fills by mid-morning. Go on a weekday or catch the first ferry of the day for a near-private island experience.
Gulf Water Temp Guide Gulf water is swimmable year-round but peak warmth (84–87°F) runs June through September. Even in January it rarely drops below 60°F — still warmer than most Atlantic beaches in summer.
Dunedin for a Non-Touristy Night Out 20 minutes north, incredibly charming, excellent craft beer, great food, and zero beach-bar crowds. A local favorite for a relaxed evening away from the tourist strip.
Indian Rocks vs. Clearwater Beach Clearwater Beach has the famous name, amenities, and crowds. Indian Rocks Beach has the quiet, the character, the easier parking, and the sunsets that are just as good. Both are wonderful.
FAQ

Clearwater Beach & Indian Rocks Beach — Frequently Asked Questions

Is Clearwater Beach worth visiting?

Absolutely. Clearwater Beach consistently ranks as one of America's top beaches, with powder-white quartz sand, warm Gulf water, and a lively strip of restaurants, water sports, and activities. The free nightly Pier 60 Sunset Celebration is a highlight you won't find anywhere else in Florida. See our full beach guide for all your options.

How far is Clearwater Beach from Tampa?

Clearwater Beach is about 45 minutes from downtown Tampa via the Courtney Campbell Causeway. Indian Rocks Beach is roughly 50 minutes from Tampa. Traffic can add 15–20 minutes during peak summer weekends — plan accordingly. See our Tampa guide for what to do before or after your beach day.

What is the best time of year to visit Clearwater Beach?

April through early June and September through November offer the best combination of warm weather, clear Gulf water, and manageable crowds. July and August are hot and very busy but the Gulf water is bathtub-warm. December through March is mild (70s°F) and popular with snowbirds escaping northern winters.

Is there free parking at Clearwater Beach?

Free parking directly on Clearwater Beach is very limited. The main Pier 60 garage charges around $3/hour. Sand Key County Park just south has a free parking lot with a beautiful beach. Indian Rocks Beach — where several of our vacation rentals are located — has much more accessible street parking, often free on side streets off Gulf Blvd.

Is Clearwater Beach family-friendly?

Yes — it's one of Florida's most family-friendly beach destinations. The calm Gulf waves are safe for young swimmers, the Clearwater Marine Aquarium is a top kids' attraction, Pier 60 has evening entertainment that's genuinely enjoyable for all ages, and dolphin boat tours create unforgettable memories. See our full family tips section above.

What is there to do at Indian Rocks Beach?

Indian Rocks Beach offers beautiful Gulf Coast swimming, waterfront dining at local favorites like Crabby Bill's and The Salty Pelican, paddleboard and kayak rentals on the Intracoastal Waterway, fishing piers, access to the 75-mile Pinellas Trail, and a relaxed Old Florida atmosphere that feels worlds away from tourist-heavy Clearwater Beach — despite being just 5 miles south.
