St. Petersburg, Florida

The Sunshine City — world-class arts, Gulf beaches, legendary sunsets, and a food scene that punches well above its weight

Where to Eat Best Beaches Activities Nightlife Getting Around Family Tips Rainy Day Local Tips FAQ

Welcome to St. Petersburg, Florida — the Sunshine City and one of the Gulf Coast's most vibrant, walkable, and genuinely surprising destinations. Downtown St. Pete has it all: the Salvador Dali Museum (the largest Dali collection outside Europe), a thriving arts district with hundreds of murals, an electric food and cocktail scene on Central Avenue, and a stunning waterfront that stretches for miles. And the beaches — St. Pete Beach, Fort De Soto, Pass-a-Grille, and Treasure Island — are all within 30 minutes.

Whether you're here for arts and culture, beach days and Gulf sunsets, craft breweries, or a combination of all three, St. Pete delivers. We're local hosts with multiple properties in St. Petersburg, and this guide is packed with everything you need to plan a perfect stay.

Where to Eat

Best Restaurants in St. Petersburg, Florida

Elegant Rooftop with Bay Views
The Birchwood
Elegant rooftop bar and restaurant with sweeping Tampa Bay views, craft cocktails, and an elevated menu. One of the most beautiful dining rooms in St. Pete — perfect for a special night out.
📍 340 Beach Dr NE, Downtown St. Pete
Mexican Cantina & Creative Cocktails
Red Mesa Cantina
Vibrant downtown cantina with inventive tacos, killer margaritas, and one of the most buzzy atmospheres in St. Pete. Great for groups and always dependably excellent.

Premier Local Food Hall
Locale Market
St. Pete's finest food hall with resident chefs, artisan butchers, a raw bar, fresh pastries, and exceptional coffee — all in a beautifully curated space. Perfect for a late breakfast or casual lunch.
📍 179 2nd Ave N, Downtown St. Pete
Italian Fine Dining
Il Ritorno
Intimate and genuinely excellent Italian with housemade pasta, impeccable service, and a wine list that rewards exploration. One of the finest restaurants in the entire Tampa Bay area.

French-American Brasserie
Cassis American Brasserie
Sophisticated brasserie on Beach Drive with a beautiful patio overlooking North Straub Park and the waterfront. The steak frites and Sunday brunch are both exceptional.
📍 170 Beach Dr NE, Downtown St. Pete
Waterfront Beach Bar

Classic Old Florida beach vibes with cold drinks, fresh grouper sandwiches, and Gulf water practically lapping at your feet. The quintessential St. Pete Beach lunch spot.
📍 6300 Gulf Blvd, St. Pete Beach

🍳 St. Pete Dining Secret

Beach Drive NE is St. Pete's most scenic dining corridor — walkable, gorgeous, and reliably great. Go on a weekday evening to get a patio table without waiting. The Saturday Morning Market (October through May) at Al Lang Field is unmissable — 150+ vendors of local food, coffee, fresh produce, and artisan goods, right on the waterfront. It's the best way to start a St. Pete Saturday.

Best Beaches Near St. Pete: Gulf Coast Sun & Sand

Classic Gulf Beach — 10 Min

One of America's most consistently rated Gulf beaches. Wide white sand, calm warm water, and a lively strip of bars and restaurants along Gulf Blvd. The closest beach to downtown St. Pete.

A quaint, unhurried historic village at the southern tip of St. Pete Beach. Quieter than the main strip with beautiful Gulf swimming and Old Florida charm that feels gloriously stuck in time.

Lively Beach Town — 15 Min
Treasure Island
Vibrant beach community with great restaurants, beach bars, and a wide sandy shore. Popular with groups and great for combining a beach day with evening entertainment options.

Where several of our rentals are! Quieter, local feel with gorgeous sunsets, walkable dining at Crabby Bill's and The Salty Pelican, and a relaxed Intracoastal vibe.

TripAdvisor's consistently top-rated US beach. Stunning white quartz sand, the famous Pier 60 Sunset Celebration, water sports, and a lively beachfront strip worth the short drive north.

🌅 St. Pete Sunset Tip

St. Pete sunsets are legendary — and you have options. The St. Pete Pier faces west and is a spectacular free sunset spot right in the city. Fort De Soto's north beach is arguably the best sunset beach in the entire state. Any Gulf-facing beach between July and September delivers unforgettable color. The closer to the Gulf you are, the better the show.

Things To Do

Top Attractions & Things to Do in St. Petersburg, FL

World-Class Art Museum
The Dali Museum
The largest Salvador Dali collection outside Europe, housed in a stunning helical staircase building on the waterfront. Mind-bending surrealist art, remarkable architecture, and an excellent cafe. A genuine must-see.
📍 1 Dali Blvd, Downtown St. Pete
Reimagined Waterfront Destination
The St. Pete Pier
St. Pete's beautifully redesigned waterfront pier with restaurants, an aquarium, kayak and paddleboard rentals, a children's area, fishing, and sweeping views of Tampa Bay. Completely free to visit.
📍 800 2nd Ave NE, Downtown St. Pete
Florida's Oldest Roadside Attraction
Sunken Gardens
One of Florida's oldest and most charming tourist attractions — lush tropical gardens with exotic plants, flamingos, koi ponds, and a beautiful winding walkway through botanical history.
📍 1825 4th St N, St. Pete
Florida's Largest Outdoor Market
Saturday Morning Market
October through May, 150+ vendors gather at Al Lang Field on the waterfront. Fresh produce, local honey, artisan crafts, food trucks, and live music every Saturday morning. A St. Pete institution.
📍 Al Lang Field, 230 1st St S — Sat 9am–2pm
Kayak, SUP & Eco Tours
Kayaking & Paddleboarding
Explore Tampa Bay's mangroves and the Gulf's barrier islands by kayak or stand-up paddleboard. Several outfitters near the St. Pete Pier offer guided tours through incredible wildlife habitat.
📍 Multiple outfitters near St. Pete Pier & Fort De Soto
5,000 Years of Art History
Museum of Fine Arts St. Pete
A remarkable collection spanning ancient Egypt to contemporary art, housed in a beautiful waterfront building on Beach Drive. Rotating exhibitions and a peaceful sculpture garden free to explore.
📍 255 Beach Dr NE, Downtown St. Pete

🎨 St. Pete Arts Tip

St. Petersburg is Florida's undisputed arts capital. The SHINE Mural Festival has produced hundreds of extraordinary murals across the city — just wander the Grand Central District (Central Ave between 16th and 31st St) and the Warehouse Arts District for a free, continuously evolving outdoor gallery. Pick up a mural map at the Visitor Center or use the St. Pete Mural Guide app for a self-guided tour.

St. Pete's most beloved local brewery with a massive outdoor beer garden, creative rotating taps, and a community feel that captures everything that makes St. Pete special.

🍸 St. Pete Nightlife Tip

The Grand Central District on Central Ave (between 16th and 31st St) is more local, more interesting, and less touristy than downtown. It's walkable, full of character, and where St. Pete residents actually go out. Uber and Lyft are easy and affordable across St. Pete — don't drive if you're exploring Central Ave or the Beach Drive bar scene. Most rides within downtown are $6–10.

Getting Around & Parking

Getting Around St. Petersburg: Transportation & Parking Tips

Bus Rapid Transit to the Beach
SunRunner Beach Bus
St. Pete's SunRunner BRT line connects downtown St. Pete to St. Pete Beach in about 30 minutes for just $2.25 per ride. Park downtown and skip the beach parking entirely — one of the region's best transit options.
📍 Multiple stops along Central Ave from downtown to the beach
Downtown Biking
BCycle Bike Share & Pinellas Trail
St. Pete's downtown is flat and genuinely bike-friendly. BCycle stations are scattered across the city center. The Pinellas Trail runs 75 miles from St. Pete all the way north to Clearwater and beyond.
📍 BCycle app — multiple stations near the Pier and Beach Drive
Downtown Parking
Metered Street Parking
Metered spots along Beach Drive and downtown run $1–2/hour. Completely free on Sundays — the best day to explore downtown by car. Parking decks on 2nd Ave N offer affordable all-day options ($5–8).
📍 2nd Ave N parking garages & metered Beach Drive spots
Beach Parking
St. Pete Beach & Treasure Island
St. Pete Beach and Treasure Island have metered parking along Gulf Blvd. Arrive before 10am on summer weekends to get a spot. The SunRunner bus is the stress-free alternative from downtown.
📍 Gulf Blvd metered lots — $2–3/hr in season
Ride-Share
Uber & Lyft
Uber and Lyft are widely available across St. Pete and neighboring beach towns. Rides within downtown typically cost $6–10. Essential for any night out on Central Avenue or the Beach Drive cocktail scene.
📱 Download both apps — surge pricing applies on weekend evenings
Highly Walkable Downtown
Downtown St. Pete on Foot
Downtown St. Pete is one of Florida's most walkable city centers. The Pier, Beach Drive, Central Ave, the Dali Museum, and the Museum of Fine Arts are all within a 20-minute walk of each other.
📍 Most major attractions within 0.5–1 mile of each other

🚌 St. Pete Transportation Tip

The SunRunner bus is genuinely one of the best transit investments in the Tampa Bay area — for $2.25 you get a direct, frequent connection from downtown St. Pete to St. Pete Beach with no parking stress. Park at the free garage near Grand Central and ride to the beach. Sunday is the best day for a downtown car-based exploration — free street parking everywhere makes it an absolute pleasure.

Family Tips

Family-Friendly St. Pete: Activities & Tips for Kids & Families

Best Family Destination
The St. Pete Pier
The pier's free children's area, small aquarium, kayak and paddleboard rentals, lawn games, ice cream, and bay views make it an ideal 2–3 hour outing for families with kids of any age.
📍 800 2nd Ave NE — free to enter; activities priced separately
Flamingos & Exotic Plants
Sunken Gardens
Kids love the flamingos, koi ponds, and the lush jungle-like walkways. An affordable, leisurely 1.5-hour outing that feels unlike anything else in Florida. Under 3 is free.
📍 1825 4th St N — very affordable entry
Fort De Soto for All Ages
Fort De Soto Park
One of Florida's finest parks with calm Gulf swimming, easy nature trails, kayak rentals, a dog beach, and a historic fort to explore. A genuine full-day family adventure just 25 minutes south.
🚗 25 min south via Pinellas Bayway — small toll applies
Family Art Experience
Dali Museum Family Programming
The Dali Museum offers dedicated family programming, scavenger hunts, and children's art-making activities on weekends. Kids are more engaged here than you'd expect — the scale and color of Dali's work is genuinely exciting for them.
📍 1 Dali Blvd — family pricing available; check website
Hands-On for Kids
Great Explorations Children's Museum
St. Pete's dedicated children's museum with imaginative, hands-on exhibits designed for ages 0–10. A rainy-day staple for local families and a reliable hit with visiting kids.
📍 1925 4th St N, St. Pete
Beach Day for Young Swimmers
St. Pete Beach or Fort De Soto
The Gulf's calm, wave-free water makes it excellent for young swimmers. St. Pete Beach is 10 minutes away with easy access. Fort De Soto's north beach is calmer, less crowded, and arguably more beautiful.
🚗 10 min to St. Pete Beach; 25 min to Fort De Soto

👨‍👩‍👧 St. Pete Family Tip

The combination of the St. Pete Pier in the morning and a Fort De Soto beach afternoon makes for a genuinely perfect family day — culture, nature, water, and wildlife all covered. Bring sunscreen, water shoes (the Gulf bottom can have shells), and a picnic. The pier has good food options but Fort De Soto has covered picnic pavilions that are great for a family lunch break during the heat of the day.

A gorgeous indoor food hall with resident chefs, a raw bar, artisan butcher, and excellent coffee. Perfect for a slow rainy morning brunch followed by browsing the artisan goods market.
📍 179 2nd Ave N, Downtown St. Pete
Live Music & Arts

St. Pete's community arts center with glass-blowing studio, rotating exhibitions, and regularly scheduled workshops. The Glass Studio hot shop demos are fascinating for all ages.

Central Avenue Covered Bar-Hopping

Rainy days are an excellent excuse to explore Central Avenue's covered bars and restaurants. Start at Mandarin Hide for cocktails, walk to The Ale and the Witch for beer, finish at Green Bench Brewing for the full St. Pete experience.

Hands-On for Kids
Great Explorations Children's Museum
A rainy day at Great Explorations is reliably excellent for kids under 10. The hands-on, imaginative exhibits keep little ones engaged for 2–3 hours while adults appreciate the air conditioning.
📍 1925 4th St N, St. Pete

⛈️ St. Pete Weather Tip

St. Pete holds the Guinness World Record for most consecutive sunny days (768 days). Rainy days are genuinely the exception. Summer afternoon storms roll through in 30–60 minutes and often produce the most dramatic, colorful sunsets immediately after. A light shower in the morning is rarely a sign of a lost beach day — check the hourly radar and stay flexible. The city rewards those who do.

Quick Local Tips

Local Secrets from Your St. Pete Hosts

Beach Drive: Go Weekday Evenings Beach Drive is stunning on weekend mornings but restaurants fill up fast. Go on a Tuesday or Wednesday evening for the same beauty and food without waiting for a table.
Dali on Thursdays After 5pm Pay-what-you-wish entry every Thursday evening after 5pm. Budget travelers and art lovers rejoice. It's less crowded than weekend visits too — a genuinely better experience.
Free Parking on Sundays Downtown street parking is completely free on Sundays. If you're driving to Beach Drive, the Pier, or the Dali Museum, Sunday is the day to do it and save the meter coins.
Saturday Market: Arrive by 9am The Saturday Morning Market (Oct–May) is best early. The most popular food vendors sell out, good spots under the palms go fast, and the energy before 10am is incomparable.
The SunRunner Bus Is the Move $2.25 from downtown to St. Pete Beach. Frequent, reliable, and eliminates beach parking entirely. Park near Grand Central free on Saturday/Sunday morning, take the bus, enjoy your day.
Fort De Soto: Go on Weekdays Fort De Soto is one of Florida's genuinely great natural places. On summer weekends the parking fills before 10am. A Thursday or Friday visit gives you practically the whole park to yourself.
Central Ave Murals: Walk West Start at downtown Central Ave and walk west toward 31st St — you're walking through one of Florida's greatest public art galleries. The Grand Central District between 16th and 31st is the best stretch.
Treasure Island for Groups St. Pete Beach gets crowded during spring break and summer weekends. Treasure Island, just one stop north on Gulf Blvd, is slightly less packed and has excellent beach bar options for groups.
Tampa Is 30 Minutes Away Ybor City, Busch Gardens, the Florida Aquarium, and the Tampa Riverwalk are all 30–35 minutes north via the Howard Frankland Bridge. See our Tampa guide for everything to do there.
FAQ

St. Petersburg, Florida — Frequently Asked Questions

Is St. Petersburg FL worth visiting?

Absolutely. St. Pete is one of Florida's most underrated destinations — a compact, walkable downtown with the Dali Museum, a thriving food scene, Central Avenue nightlife, and Gulf beaches just 10–30 minutes away. It's a city that consistently surprises visitors with how much it has to offer, and how genuinely livable and beautiful it feels.

How far is St. Pete from the beach?

St. Pete Beach is about 10–15 minutes from downtown St. Petersburg. Pass-a-Grille Beach is 20 minutes, Treasure Island is 15 minutes, and Fort De Soto Park is 25 minutes south. Clearwater Beach — America's #1 rated beach — is 35 minutes north. Use the SunRunner bus ($2.25) to get to St. Pete Beach without worrying about parking.

What is St. Pete known for?

St. Pete is known for the Salvador Dali Museum (the largest Dali collection outside Europe), a thriving arts scene with hundreds of SHINE Festival murals, Central Avenue nightlife and craft breweries, the St. Pete Pier, Fort De Soto Park, the legendary Saturday Morning Market, and holding the Guinness World Record for most consecutive sunny days — 768.

Is St. Pete family-friendly?

Very much so. The St. Pete Pier has a children's area, kayak rentals, and waterfront play spaces. Sunken Gardens delights kids with flamingos and exotic plants. Fort De Soto has excellent beaches and nature trails for all ages. Great Explorations Children's Museum is purpose-built for younger kids. See our full family tips section above.

What is the best time to visit St. Petersburg FL?

October through May is prime season in St. Pete — warm days, low humidity, and perfectly clear Gulf water. The Saturday Morning Market runs this period too. Summer (June–September) is hot and occasionally stormy in the afternoons, but the Gulf beaches are warm and beautiful and crowds are manageable except for July 4th weekend. St. Pete averages around 360 sunny days per year.

How far is St. Pete from Tampa?

St. Petersburg is about 25–35 minutes from downtown Tampa via the Howard Frankland or Gandy Bridge, depending on traffic. The two cities together make an excellent combined vacation base — Tampa's Ybor City, Busch Gardens, and the Florida Aquarium pair perfectly with St. Pete's arts, beaches, and waterfront.
